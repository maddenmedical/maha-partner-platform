# Community Chat — Purpose Copy

## Tagline (nav / header subtitle)
Your global network of MAHA partners.

## Persistent banner (top of the Community page, always visible via an "i"/info affordance — not a chat message, so it never gets buried under posts)
This is where MAHA partners from around the world come together — to talk through real cases, share the techniques and philosophies that work in their hands, and see how others approach the same problem differently. It's also where the relationships that turn into referrals and lasting friendships get started.

## What's this for? (expandable list — pairs with lucide icons in the UI, no emoji)
- **Brainstorm real cases** — post a tricky one and get perspectives from clinicians who've seen it before.
- **Learn how the world does it** — every country and training background brings a different approach. See what you'd never see inside your own practice.
- **Build your network** — the partners you meet here become referral partners, co-speakers, and collaborators.
- **Talk about where the field is going** — new techniques, new materials, new ways of running a practice.

## Framing line for the new-member intro dialog (short, sits above the message/video/link fields)
Tell your new partners a bit about yourself — this is where cases get discussed, techniques get shared, and the MAHA network gets stronger.
